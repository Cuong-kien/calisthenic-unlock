Gamified Training

Every Rep Counts Toward Your Next Unlock

Planche turns calisthenics into a progression game. Earn XP, collect badges, and watch your skill tree light up.

Collect Skill Badges

Each skill has a unique badge. Locked badges are greyed out. Hit the hold-time goal and watch it light up with full color.

Progressive Skill Trees

Skills are organized in unlock paths. Master Frog Stand before tackling Tuck Planche. Each unlock opens new challenges.

Hold-Time Progress

Your progress bar fills as you increase your hold time. Hit the target seconds and the skill is officially unlocked.

Multiple Skill Groups

Planche, Handstand, Front Lever, Dragon Flag and more. Each group has its own badge collection and progression path.

Streak Calendar

Build training streaks and keep your momentum going. Consistency is the key to unlocking harder skills.

Custom Difficulty

Adjust reps, sets, and hold durations for each exercise. Scale the challenge to match your current level.
